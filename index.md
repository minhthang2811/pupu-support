# Pupu

A day planner for iPhone, with a puppy. Pupu organizes a day into loose
time-of-day buckets — Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening — instead of a rigid
timetable, keeps your routines and a to-do list, and nudges you with reminders.

Pupu himself lives in the app. Play with him and he learns tricks; finish things
and he gets treats; finish three in a day and he goes exploring, then sends you
a postcard.

Everything you enter stays on your device. In English and Tiếng Việt.
