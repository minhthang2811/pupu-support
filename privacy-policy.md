# Privacy Policy for Pupu

**Last updated: 22 October 2026**

Pupu is a day planner that runs entirely on your device.

## What Pupu collects

**Nothing.** Pupu has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. It makes no
network requests of any kind. No data about you or your use of the app is
transmitted anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

## What Pupu stores, and where

Everything you enter, and everything Pupu remembers, is written to a private
storage area inside the app's own sandbox on your iPhone, readable only by
Pupu. That includes your activities, to-dos, routines and reminder settings;
Pupu's friendship hearts, the tricks he has learned and the postcards he has
sent you; your language, appearance, sound and haptics choices; and when you
last opened the app, so he can say hello the right way.

That data is removed when you delete the app. If you have iCloud Backup enabled,
it may be included in your device backup; that backup is governed by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not ours.

## Icon suggestions

Pupu picks an icon for an activity from the words you type, using a list of
keywords built into the app. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, it may also
ask Apple's on-device language model for a suggestion. That model runs entirely
on your iPhone: the text is not sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Pupu schedules **local notifications** — they are
created on your device by your device, for activities with a start time and
to-dos with a due time that you yourself entered, and (unless you switch it
off) one when Pupu gets home from an adventure. No push notification service is
involved and no device token is ever generated or sent.

You can revoke notification permission at any time in
Settings → Pupu → Notifications, or turn reminders off inside Pupu.

## Device language

Pupu reads your device's language setting so it can start in English or
Vietnamese without asking. That value is read locally and never leaves the
device. If you pick a language inside Pupu, that choice is stored on the device
too.

## Links to this website

Me → About in the app has links to this privacy policy and to the support
page. Tapping one opens Safari; Pupu itself sends nothing. This website is
hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives the information any website receives
when you visit it, such as your IP address, under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Tracking

Pupu does not track you. It contains no advertising identifiers, no third-party
SDKs that collect data, and nothing that links your activity to other apps or
websites.

## Children

Pupu collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
