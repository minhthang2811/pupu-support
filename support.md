## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone only. Pupu makes no network requests and has no account system.
See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

**My reminders stopped arriving.**
Check Settings → Pupu → Notifications on your iPhone. If notification permission
was turned off there, Pupu switches its reminders off and says so on
Me → Reminders. Turning them back on there schedules everything again.

**What can have a reminder?**
Activities with a start time, and to-dos with a due time — up to six reminders
each. An activity parked in a time-of-day bucket with no clock time has no
moment to fire at, so it does not get one.

**How does Pupu go on an adventure?**
Finish three things in a day — activities or to-dos both count — and he sets
off. He comes home about 30 minutes later with a postcard for your album on Me.
One trip a day.

**How does Pupu learn tricks?**
Through friendship hearts: the first time you play with him each day, each treat
you give him, and each postcard he brings home. Hearts never go down. The tricks
he knows are on Me; tap one and he shows you.

**How do treats work?**
Each thing you finish puts a treat in his bowl on Me, up to three a day. Drag it
to him.

**How do I change the language?**
Me → Language. Pupu follows your iPhone's language by default, and you can pick
English or Tiếng Việt there instead.

**Will my data survive deleting the app?**
No. Deleting Pupu removes its storage with it.
